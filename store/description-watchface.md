A Pebble Time 2 (emery) watchface inspired by the Apple Watch Solar face. It plots the sun's elevation over the day for your location and shows a glowing sun at the current time.

- The curve runs from midnight (left) to midnight (right). The grey line is the horizon.
- The glow changes with the sun's elevation: blue during the day, red and pink at the horizon, and a hollow ring when the sun is below the horizon.
- Your location comes from the phone and is cached on the watch.
- The face redraws once per minute.

Best used together with the companion app Solar Times: https://apps.repebble.com/39857a1cdd1d42aa9313294f

Watchfaces can't react to buttons or touch, so the interactive part lives in Solar Times. It shows when the sun rises, peaks at noon and sets today, and how far away each of those is from now. Assign Solar Times to a button in Settings → Quick Launch to open it straight from this watchface; Back takes you back to the face.
